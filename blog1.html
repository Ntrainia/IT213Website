Train’s Corner: What does the author have to say today???



How to find inspiration to create?

Many ask me, "Miss Author, how do you do it? How do you create such masterpieces and publish them at an astounding rate?" The answer is...I don't know. I am just that great. Sometimes I amaze myself. To find inspiration is like looking for a pot of gold at the rainbow, you won't find it because it isn't there. In my belief, it is not inspiration that people need. It is the discipline to study the world around you.

For me, I have always lived a rather plain life. I had let life passed me by; my childhood, my preteen years, my teenager years, and my early twenties seemed to flew by in a flash with nothing noteworthy to claim as my own. So for those of you out there who seemed lost, don't worry I was in your shoes once as well.

Inspiration is everywhere for those who are willing to take a closer look. Everyone has a personality. We all have things we love or hate, even if we don't deem our interests as worthy of respect. Learn to love your interests and expend your horizons. Connect the dots and slowly your web will grow. Inspiration is there. But are you willing to conquer your fear of creating and making something for yourself without the fear of others judgements or your own criticism?

For what it is worth, write something small. Anything at all. Get a pencil and a sheet of paper, write some sentences, and you'll have created a world just like that. Learn to love your creations as if it was your own child. Don't spoil them by thinking they're perfect, but correct their flaws so that they can that they can grow to be better. Remember to try your best and remember to have fun!
