The Bald Man and His Magical Balloon Book 1



The Adventure of the Bald Man and His Balloon: Top Secret Mission

On an average day, the Bald Man and His Balloon were playing around the park when they spotted strange people in suits. How strange for these men and women to be dressed in all black in stuffy suits when it was such a sunny day. Being the curious blokes they were, the Bald Man and his Balloon decided to sneakily follow these people. They thought they were being plenty quiet and secretiv until they found themselves in an empty alley. SUDDENLY, they were pushed into the back of a moving van where they find themselves standing in front of the people dressed in black suits. "Bald Man. Balloon. We are the CIBB. We need your help."

Find out whether or not the Bald Man and His Balloon will succeed in their top secret mission in The Adventure of the Bald Man and His Balloon: Top Secret Mission! Will the duo survive? Can the CIBB be trusted? Who is truly the enemy? Find out when you buy it!
