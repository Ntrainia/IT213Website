About the Author: Knock Train 

Knock Train was born in a small local hospital somewhere in the middle of nowhere in the upper region of an United States' state with the acronym that starts with a V and ends with an A, known as Virginia. She was an average student and an average writer, but that never stopped her from trying to become an author. She always believed that her greatest skill was her creativity, which she quote "Kept me living day by day."  

She started dwindling into writing in elementary school, finding joy in creative writing where she wrote silly stories in hopes that her teachers would laugh. She expanded her collection of personal works in middle school where she wrote fanfiction for her cousins and friends, though she had an awful habit of not completing her stories.

She went to Northern Virginia Community College as an IT major. She graduated and transferred to George Mason University. School and personal life issues kept her from writing for a while, until that eventful day in the cafe where the popping of a balloon sparked her drive to write, leading up to the creation of the beloved series "The Adventure of the Bald Man and His Balloon."

Now she lives in Anarctica with a family of penguins. She sold over  500 million books. She won many awards for her works. Her hope is to continue writing and spreading the joy of reading to kids across the world.
