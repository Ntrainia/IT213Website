About the Author: Knock Train 

Knock Train was born in a small local hospital somewhere in the middle of nowhere in the upper region of an United States' state with the acronym that starts with a V and ends with an A, known as Virginia. She was an average student and an average writer, but that never stopped her from trying. She always believed that her greatest skill was her creativity, which she quote "Kept me living day by..."

About the Series: The Adventure of the Bald Man and His Balloon 

Our author Knock Train was at a cafe with her father and mother when the sun was shining brightly through the window. Our author didn't realize how beautiful the day was until it quite literally reflected off her father's bald head. She found herself in a trance until a balloon popped in the cafe, making her jump.
