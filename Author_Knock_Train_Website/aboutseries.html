About the Series: The Adventure of the Bald Man and His Balloon  

Our author Knock Train was at a cafe with her father and mother when the sun was shining brightly through the window. Our author didn't realize how beautiful the day was until it quite literally reflected off her father's bald head. She found herself in a trance until a balloon popped in the cafe, making her jump in her chair. She knew she had a book idea right there and then.

To some people, it may come to be a surprise that The Adventure of the Bald Man and His Balloon was rejected by many publishers. After all, who wanted a book about a bald man and some balloon? But Knock Train persisted, going to publisher after publisher, because she believed that the story was worth reading.

It was until she decided to self publish her first book, which became an overnight sensation. Kids all over the world were eager to read about the Bald Man and His Balloon. Balloon sales were over the ceiling. Many clowns consider the book series their saving grace, giving the series credit for saving the clown industry.

As of now, the series has sold over 500 million copies worldwide. The series is translated in 11 languages. It had became such a sucess that Knock Train created her own publishing house known as Train Publishing House.
